How it works (this is the real math, byte-honest):
each phone computes EUCLIDEAN distance to every visible neighbor: d(phoneA,phoneB) = √((Δx)²+(Δy)²). The mesh keeps phones within range connected as an EDGE (blue line). The seeker finds the SOS by its GRADIENT: traverse the neighbor whose hop-count toward SOS is LOWEST — not necessarily the straightest Euclidean line, because a wall/obstacle can make the straight hop unreachable. That's why the paper calls it "gradient navigation," not "beeline." Drag the SOS to one side and the seeker far away: the highlighted RISK path is the hop-minimum route — the one real phones would take.
